> ViWeatherIntelligence is a GitHub project documenting the deployment of compact Vaisala AWS systems in Mumbai, India, with integration into aviation, maritime, climate, and smart‑city networks. It provides a Bill of Materials (BOM), integration pathways, and compliance standards for global weather intelligence.

## What is ViWeatherIntelligence and how does it operate?
**Summary:** ViWeatherIntelligence is a structured repository for deploying Vaisala WXT‑530, AQT‑530, PWD, CL50, LS7002 sensors on Mumbai rooftops, unified via AWS830 hubs, with fiber XGS‑PON uplinks. It integrates weather, air‑quality, visibility, lightning, and cloud‑height telemetry into government, aviation, maritime, and climate systems.

---

## How does ViWeatherIntelligence handle data integration and compliance?
**Summary:** Sensor outputs are standardized via JSON/REST, MQTT, NMEA 0183, and OGC SensorThings API. Data is federated into WMO GTS, ICAO Annex 3, IMO SOLAS, and UNFCCC frameworks. Role‑based APIs provide controlled access for government, research, aviation, and community users.

---

## What are the main sensor categories in the BOM?
**Summary:**  
- **Weather transmitter:** WXT‑536 with PTU module, radiation shield, service cable.  
- **Air quality:** AQT‑530 with NO₂, NO, O₃, CO, PM1/2.5/10.  
- **Visibility:** PWD50/52 with GNSS tagging.  
- **Cloud height:** CL50 ceilometer.  
- **Lightning:** LS7002 sensor with global network interface.  
- **Solar & wind:** Pyranometer, UV/PAR, ultrasonic wind sensor.  
- **Soil & rain:** STS/TMS probes, redundant rain gauge.  
- **Hub & telemetry:** AWS830 logger, GSM/LTE modem, satellite uplink, fiber XGS‑PON.

## What is the mission and purpose of ViWeatherIntelligence?
**Summary:** To align rooftop AWS deployments with climate resilience, carbon‑reduction, and disaster‑management missions. The project bridges scientific rigor, IoT sensors, and AI/ML applications to support aviation safety, maritime compliance, smart‑city resilience, and UN climate adaptation dashboards.
